Our story

Farm fresh, made easy

Dero Greens is a Nairobi-based producer of pre-cooked, dried traditional African leafy vegetables — managu, terere, saga, mrenda, kunde, malenge, kanzira and mitoo — sold as single varieties and signature blends.

We serve two audiences deliberately: diaspora and heritage buyers who already know these vegetables and want the taste of home, and premium wellness buyers discovering plant-based, superfood-style eating for the first time. Locally, our vegetables also reach households, supermarkets, hospitality and institutions — though for now this site focuses on our export and diaspora business.

Operated by Kito Kubuni, trading as Dero Greens.
