A taste of home

Reducing food loss. Empowering women. Preserving Africa's traditional vegetables.

For generations, these vegetables have been treasured in African households — not only for their distinctive taste, but for their exceptional nutritional benefits. At Dero Greens, we believe some of the world's most nutritious foods have yet to be discovered by the world.

Rooted in the rich agricultural heritage of Kenya, we transform traditional African leafy vegetables into premium, convenient dried and frozen products for today's health-conscious consumer — naturally grown, hygienically processed, and preserved to retain their natural flavour, colour and nutritional value. We are not simply exporting vegetables. We are sharing Africa's nutritional heritage, one plate at a time.

Operated by Kito Kubuni, trading as Dero Greens.

As a women-led Kenyan agribusiness with a 90%+ women workforce, Dero Greens is proud to connect local farmers with international markets — reducing post-harvest losses, promoting sustainable agriculture, and making healthy food more accessible across the world. At Dero Greens, we contribute to:

  • Reduction of post-harvest losses through value addition and preservation
  • Increased market access for women farmers
  • Women's economic empowerment through employment and enterprise opportunities
  • Improved food and nutrition security through access to nutrient-rich traditional vegetables
  • Promotion and preservation of indigenous African food systems
  • Sustainable and climate-resilient agriculture through responsible farming practices

Our markets

International market — serving African diaspora communities and global consumers seeking authentic African foods with long shelf life, convenience and nutritional value.

A local Kenya market also exists in our business but is intentionally not built out as a separate site path right now, as we focus on export.

Our vision

To become a leading African food company that preserves indigenous vegetables, empowers women and farming communities, and builds sustainable food systems for local and global markets.

Our mission

To reduce food loss, increase farmer incomes, and deliver convenient, nutritious traditional African vegetables through sustainable processing and market-driven innovation.
