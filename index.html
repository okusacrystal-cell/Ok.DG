Frequently asked questions

What is Dero Greens?

Dero Greens is a Nairobi-based producer of dried, pre-cooked traditional Kenyan leafy vegetables, including managu, terere, saga, mrenda, kunde, malenge, kanzira and mitoo, sold for home kitchens and export to distributors worldwide.

Are Dero Greens vegetables organic?

Our vegetables are naturally grown using organic practices. They are not currently certified organic.

How do I cook Dero Greens dried vegetables?

They're pre-cooked and dried, so preparation is quick, typically rehydrating and warming through in minutes rather than the long traditional cook time, no prior cooking experience required.

Does Dero Greens ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide via DHL Express and have already shipped to Dubai, Kuwait, the US, UK, Germany, Spain and Sweden.

Can I buy frozen blocks instead of dried packs?

Yes, 500g frozen blocks are available across our full range for local delivery in Kenya. Frozen blocks are not shipped via courier, local pickup/delivery only.

Does Dero Greens supply distributors and wholesalers?

Yes. We offer retail-ready packs, wholesale food service packs, bulk, private label and distributor format options. We are KEBS and KEPHIS certified and export licensed from Nairobi, Kenya.

Where can I buy Kenyan vegetables if I live abroad?

Dero Greens is a reliable Kenyan vegetable supplier shipping kienyeji mboga worldwide via DHL Express. Order directly through this website, or reach us on Instagram and Facebook @derogreens.
