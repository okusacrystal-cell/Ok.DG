Two buyer paths

Already familiar with African indigenous vegetables?

Serve your diaspora customers the vegetables they grew up with, managu, terere, saga, mrenda, kunde, malenge, kanzira, mitoo, plus signature blends, shelf-stable, pre-cooked, and ready in minutes.

New to this category? Think "premium wellness foods."
