Recipes & usage

Ready in minutes, however you serve it

Every Dero Greens vegetable is pre-cooked before it's dried — kienyeji vegetables the traditional way, without the traditional prep time. Rehydrate, warm through, and it's ready: alongside ugali, rice, a stew, or folded into a stir-fry.

How to prepare

Three steps, every vegetable

  1. Soak in hot water for 10 minutes
  2. Fry with onions & tomatoes for 3–5 minutes
  3. Serve with ugali, rice, or chapati — or see the ideas below for something different

Storage: keep tightly sealed in an airtight container. Shelf life up to 24 months.
